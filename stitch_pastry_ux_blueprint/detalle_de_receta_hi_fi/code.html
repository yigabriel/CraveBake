Rustic Sourdough Boule

A deeply flavored, naturally leavened country loaf with a crisp, blistered crust and a tender, open crumb. The epitome of artisanal baking.

Ingredients

  • check
    400g Bread Flour (12% protein minimum)
  • check
    100g Whole Wheat Flour
  • check
    375g Filtered Water (at 80°F/27°C)
  • check
    100g Active Sourdough Starter (100% hydration)
  • check
    10g Fine Sea Salt

kitchen Required Equipment

Dutch Oven Banneton Basket Lame or Razor Digital Scale

Process Overview

  1. 1

    Autolyse

    Mix flours and water until no dry spots remain. Rest for 1 hour to hydrate the starches and begin gluten development.

  2. 2

    Incorporate Starter & Salt

    Dimple in the starter, mix. Rest 30 mins. Dimple in salt with a splash of water. Mix until cohesive.
